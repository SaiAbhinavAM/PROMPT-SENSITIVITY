01 — The Problem

Why Does This Research Matter?

🚨 The Core Problem

When you ask an AI the same thing in different ways, it should give equally good answers. But current evaluation tools like ROUGE can't tell the difference between a model that truly fails and a model that gives a perfectly good answer using different words.

📝

Prompt 1

"Summarize this article."

ROUGE: 0.42 ✅
📝

Prompt 2

"Give me a brief overview."

ROUGE: 0.19 ❌
🧠

Reality

Both answers are equally good. ROUGE just penalizes different words.

Evaluation Artifact
Our Research Goal
Build a framework that correctly evaluates model robustness — distinguishing genuine failure from surface-level metric noise.

04 — Phase 2 · ORI

Observational Robustness Index — 4 Pillars

SMS

Semantic Manifold Stability

Converts all 12 outputs to vector embeddings. Measures how "spread out" meanings are using Wasserstein Distance.

S_SMS = 1 − W₁(Base, Variants)
🎯 Low spread = high semantic stability
AUC-E

Performance Elasticity

Uses LLM-as-a-Judge (scored 0–1) to measure how fast quality drops across the 3 prompt levels.

Area Under Elasticity Curve
🎯 High AUC = quality stays high throughout
TRD

Topic-Response Divergence

Uses Jensen-Shannon Divergence in shared topic space to check if responses drift off-topic.

S_TRD = 1 − D_JS(Prompt ‖ Response)
🎯 Low divergence = stays on topic
KPIG

Instructional Stability

Checks if model follows a key constraint (e.g., "50 words") when it's rephrased 10 different ways. Catches "half-listening" models.

S_KPIG = 1 − σ²(IG)
🎯 Low variance = understands constraint
05 — Phase 3 · IFI

Intrinsic Fidelity Index — Opening the Hood

While ORI watches the outputs, IFI looks inside the model to ask — is this model genuinely confident, or is it statistically confused?

📊
PPL_var — Perplexity Variance

Perplexity = how "surprised" the model is by a prompt. We calculate it for all 12 variants and measure the standard deviation.

Low σ = model finds all phrasings equally natural = truly robust
High σ = model is confused by some phrasings = brittle
🎯 Analogy: A fluent English speaker reads "summarize" and "give an overview" with equal ease
🌡️
PC_stab — Probability Concentration

At each word generated, we measure the model's Branching Factor — how many word options it's seriously considering.

Low BF = confident, knows what to say
High BF = confused, considering many options
Unstable BF = sensitive to phrasing
🎯 Analogy: A fluent speaker doesn't pause before every word. An uncertain one does.

IFI requires white-box model access (token probabilities). Evaluated on open-source models: LLaMA-3, Mistral-7B, Falcon-7B. For closed-source models (GPT-4, Claude), ORI alone serves as a black-box estimator.

06 — Phase 4 · Final Score

The Prompt Robustness Index — PRI

PRI = Σwᵢ / Σ(wᵢ/Sᵢ)

Weighted Harmonic Mean of all 6 pillar scores

S₁
SMS — Semantic Manifold Stability
S₂
AUC-E — Performance Elasticity
S₃
TRD — Topic-Response Divergence
S₄
KPIG — Instructional Stability
S₅
PPL_var — Perplexity Variance
S₆
PC_stab — Probability Concentration

Why Harmonic Mean?

The harmonic mean punishes catastrophic failure. If a model scores 0.95 on 5 pillars but 0.05 on one, the harmonic mean pulls the final score down dramatically — unlike a simple average which would hide the weakness.

Car Safety Analogy

Your car's safety score shouldn't be "great" just because 4 of 5 systems work. If the brakes fail, that matters disproportionately. PRI captures this.

⚠️ Implementation Note (Current Limitation)

The LLM Judge used in this phase was Flan-T5-base (250M parameters) — a small model. Published research shows small models have lower human correlation (~0.35) vs. larger judges like GPT-4o (~0.82). 🚨 TRD Problem

TRD shows negative correlation with human scores. This means it's measuring the opposite of what humans value — likely because it penalizes models that naturally expand context. Solution: Remove TRD from PRI in next iteration.

✅ Final Score at 0.602

The composite Final Score achieves Pearson r = 0.602 — the strongest result. This validates that multi-dimensional scoring outperforms any single metric. Model ranking (3/4 correct) also confirms ordinal validity.

⚠️ Why Correlations Are Moderate

Root cause: Flan-T5-base judge (250M params) compresses human score range to 0.56–0.64, artificially limiting all correlations. Expected to reach r ≥ 0.70 with proper judge.

12 — Limitations & Fixes

Current Limitations & Planned Fixes

🚨 Identified Issues

1
Weak LLM Judge (Flan-T5-base)
250M parameter model cannot reliably evaluate quality. Produces compressed scores (0.56–0.64), limiting correlation ceiling.
2
TRD Negatively Correlated
Both TRD variants show r = -0.245 to -0.314. Metric design needs revision or complete removal.
3
KPIG Underperforming
Scores near 0.12–0.15 for all models. Formula needs rigorous mathematical reformulation using KL divergence.
4
PRI Weights Unjustified
Equal weights assumed. Need ablation study or learned weights for scientific validity.

✅ Planned Fixes

Fix 1 — Replace Judge
Flan-T5-base → GPT-4o-mini / Mistral-7B
Expected: correlation r improves from 0.38 → 0.65+
Fix 2 — Remove TRD
New PRI = f(SMS, AUC-E, KPIG, PPL_var, PC_stab)
Recompute PRI without TRD. Expected: PRI correlation improves.
Fix 3 — SummEval Correlation
Correlate SMS against existing human labels
Adds external validation without new human study.
Fix 4 — Weight Ablation
Test 3 weight configs → show PRI is stable
Proves robustness to weight choice = a strength.
13 — Novelty & Contributions

Genuine Novel Contributions

✅ Proven by Data

Evaluation Artifact — Empirically Proven

We show ROUGE underestimates model quality by 68–165% across 4 models. This is a concrete, citable finding that challenges how the field uses ROUGE for open-ended tasks.

✅ Novel Approach

Dual-Index Design (ORI + IFI)

First framework to separate behavioral robustness (ORI) from mechanistic robustness (IFI) in open-ended generation. PromptBench does not do this.

✅ New Problem Definition

KPIG — "Half-Listening" Problem

First formalized measurement of whether models follow instruction meaning vs. surface keywords. Our results confirm all 4 models half-listen (KPIG ≈ 0.12–0.15).

✅ Practical Warning

Small Judge = Unreliable Evaluation

We demonstrate that LLM judge model size critically confounds results. A key methodological warning for future evaluation papers — itself a citable finding.

Positioning Statement
This work is positioned as the first systematic robustness study specifically designed for open-ended generation. PromptBench (2023) covers classification/NLU. HELM covers general evaluation. Our gap: open-ended generation + dual behavioral/mechanistic analysis.
